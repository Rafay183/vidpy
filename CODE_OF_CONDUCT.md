# Code of Conduct

vidpy welcomes everyone — students, video creators, engineers and first-time contributors from every country and background.

## Our standards

**Please do**
* Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers.
* Give constructive feedback about code, not about people.
* Assume good intent and ask questions before judging.
* Credit other people's work and ideas.

**Not acceptable**
* Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
* Personal or political attacks.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Spam, or repeatedly pushing unrelated promotion.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the vidpy project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, Muhammad Rafay Shaikh, through a GitHub direct contact or by opening a private security advisory on the repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
